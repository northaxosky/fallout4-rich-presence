$RichPresence_FaqBlankImage	Why is one of my images blank?
$RichPresence_FaqBlankImageAnswer	The image key has not been uploaded to the Discord application, or the key was typed incorrectly. Keys are 1-32 lowercase letters, digits, and underscores.
$RichPresence_FaqPrivacy	How do I hide my location or quest?
$RichPresence_FaqPrivacyAnswer	Use the Privacy group on the Settings page, or install the Spoiler-free preset.
$RichPresence_FaqRuntimes	Which Fallout 4 runtimes are supported?
$RichPresence_FaqRuntimesAnswer	One DLL supports 1.10.163, 1.10.984, 1.11.221, and 1.11.240.
$RichPresence_FaqStorage	Where are my settings stored?
$RichPresence_FaqStorageAnswer	Data/F4SE/Plugins/Fallout4RichPresence.toml is the installed preset. Put personal overrides in Fallout4RichPresenceCustom.toml beside it so they survive reinstalling.
